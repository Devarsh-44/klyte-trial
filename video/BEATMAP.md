# Klyte 2.0, the hydration update: beat map

**Song:** "What About Action?" by Diego Nava (Mixkit), cut to 15 bars and retimed to **120 BPM**, so 1 beat = 0.5 s and 60 beats = 30.0 s.
**Canvas:** 1440 × 1440, warm off-white `#F4F1EA`, black UI. Archivo (wdth 125, wght 800) for the wordmark, Geist for UI.
**Rule:** something happens on every beat. Nothing fades, blurs or cuts; every scene is made out of the previous one.

Music: beats 0–11 build · **12 drop** · 12–43 groove · 44–55 quiet breakdown (no kick) · **56 drop returns**.

## Uncommon techniques

1. **Pour-flood**: the black flood is a liquid surface rising (a spring-driven slosh that is still a pure function of time), not a flat expanding shape.
2. **Pull-tab iris**: a six-blade aperture whose blades are can-lid tabs pivoting on rivets.
3. **Accordion wordmark**: letters squeeze into their own period through the variable width axis.
